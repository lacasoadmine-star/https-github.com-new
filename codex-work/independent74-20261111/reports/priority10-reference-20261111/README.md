- Multi Hot 10 and 20 show five columns, three rows and an upper multiplier arc. Their separately rendered paytables differ. Actual wins remain pending until collected, a new spin is accepted, or the documented X2 flow is used. The observed manual collections credited the displayed amounts exactly.
- The lightning control opens an explicit Quick Play preference dialog. Enabling it changes the lightning and spin arrows to yellow while leaving the balance unchanged. An earlier inference that it directly starts a spin was withdrawn after the later dialog and history evidence.
- Burn Wilds shows a two-seven award of 0.05 DMO at a total stake of 0.10 DMO. The model therefore needs the published half-line prize divisor; the client displays that ratio without changing server-paid amounts. No Wild expansion occurred in the five fresh Burn rounds.
- Sugar Fiesta is a three-column, three-row game with nine regular categories, including five food/candy symbols and four letters. Its observed public DMO table is retained with the geometry capture.
- Gates of Merlyn's rendered paytable resolves the earlier static placeholder values. The upper and lower captures show all nine regular categories, thresholds of 8–9 / 10–11 / 12+, and scatter awards. Its five crystal categories are red, purple, yellow, green and white. Multiplier badges are blue for 2–9, purple for 10–40, and red for 50–500 using the explicitly enumerated values.

- Book of Bonanza has a six-column, four-row Chinese temple board and forty explicitly drawn paylines. The rendered table identifies Emperor, Dragon, Lotus, Gong, Lantern and five card categories, with Book as Wild/scatter. The captured chart and table correct the prior independent twenty-line definition. Help still describes a three-position expansion window on the four-row board; no fresh bonus trigger resolved that ambiguity.

The later Sugar Fiesta follow-up reported a one-table session limit while browser inventory contained only that official game, the existing Player tab and a blank tab. A bounded recovery observation timed out, and the tab was closed without Play. Its five-round quota remains unfinished.
