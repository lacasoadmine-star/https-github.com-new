How to play

Five reels, three rows and 20 fixed paylines. Matching symbols pay from left to right, starting on the first reel. Only the highest win on each line is paid.

The wheel selects a multiplier from 1× to 5×. All winning lines are added, then multiplied by the wheel result. The maximum win is 1,000× your total bet.

This game has no wild symbols, scatter symbols or free-spin round.

X2 and TAKE WIN

After an eligible win, choose red or black to double the displayed winnings. An incorrect choice loses that amount. TAKE WIN keeps your current winnings. Limits appear in the X2 screen.

Play controls

STOP finishes the current reel animation sooner. Quick play uses faster animations. Choose an autoplay count and optional stop limits, then press START PLAY. Press AUTO again to stop future spins.

If your connection is interrupted, use Recover round to restore the result. An accepted spin remains valid. Autoplay stops when you leave the game.

ZPLAY uses an independent game model. These rules describe this ZPLAY game. Paytable preview does not change your selected bet.
