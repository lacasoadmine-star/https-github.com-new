The key symbol is the
crown Wild
, which can appear anywhere on the reels and substitute for other symbols to form winning combinations. When a Wild lands on the grid but does not take part in a win, it remains fixed in its position while a new spin begins.
With every new spin in which that Wild does not participate in a winning combination, its multiplier increases by +1, growing from x2 to x3, x4, and beyond. The multiplier continues to charge as long as the Wild stays on the grid without contributing to a win.
When a Wild becomes part of a winning combination, the win is paid and multiplied by the Wild’s current multiplier value. If a win occurs without using a Wild symbol, the payout is awarded according to the paytable and the spin continues, allowing existing Wilds to keep increasing their multipliers.
Multiple Wilds can appear on the reels at the same time, each building its own multiplier independently. Wilds that participate in a winning combination are removed from the grid, while those that do not remain in place and continue increasing their multipliers with each new spin.
