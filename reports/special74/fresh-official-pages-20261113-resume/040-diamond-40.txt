Samurai is inspired by the legends of feudal Japan, combining traditional visuals with proven bonus features. Expanding wilds and a free spins round bring volatility and reward potential to the forefront, while the game’s art direction and sound design create an immersive setting. Samurai offers a culturally themed, visually polished title that fits seamlessly into a broad portfolio.
