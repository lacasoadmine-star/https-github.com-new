MineIsland 2
is a single-player crash game where the player guides a Kangaroo jumping from one floating island to the next. Each island carries a fixed multiplier, and the higher the Kangaroo climbs, the bigger the win and the greater the risk that an island crumbles before the player collects.
‍
Specifications:
The sequel keeps the classic Mine Island mechanic and adds a Boost that multiplies all remaining prizes by 1.5x to 2.0x, plus a Portal that can rescue the Kangaroo after a fall. Three volatility levels let the player choose the risk, with wins of up to 10,000x on Hard.
‍
Game Features:
3 volatility levels
10 multiplier steps
Boost multiplier up to 2x
Portal second-chance rescue
Autoplay with Auto Collect
Player-paced gameplay
